BTW, I've analyzed TMs with blocks that has similar behavior. And what I've noticed is that you can pair some blocks so that they're "linked" in some sense. For instance, when looking at 01 03 <A or 11 03 <A, the head bounces between the two blocks and comes out to the left as <A 22 21 and <A 22 11, respectively. And when you replace the <A at the final step with A>, you return to what it was originally.

The thing is, you can link blocks "remotely" as well. For example, in 01 11 03 03 <A. You can pair the 11 03 in the middle so you have 01 (11 03) 03 <A, but since the pair in the middle always lets the head pass through both directions, we can imagine it's not even there, and we can then pair the outer 01 and 03, so we have (01 (11 03) 03) <A

Extending to 01 01 03 01 03 03 <A, we can pair them like (01 (01 03) (01 03) 03). Just make sure the pairs are in the correct order. It won't work if you have 03 01 or 03 11. You need to pair them so that they bounce between each other.
