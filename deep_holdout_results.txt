Deep holdout screens - 2026-11-06
=================================

Driver: bbwork/deep_screen.py.  It partitions a standard-format list, runs
sim.exe on 16 workers, and rejects a run unless exactly one CSV row per
machine is returned.  "No halt" here is a lower bound at the stated cap; it
is not a decision and says nothing about the BB function.

Commands
--------
py -3.14 deep_screen.py --list bb6_specs.txt --cap 1000000000 --jobs 16
py -3.14 deep_screen.py --list Bb2x5_informalLast37.txt --cap 10000000000 --jobs 16
py -3.14 deep_screen.py --list BB2x5_Rocq_holdouts_60.txt --cap 10000000000 --jobs 16 --strip-id

Interpretation
--------------
- No new holdout was decided.  Every machine survived its cap.
- The 37-machine hard core scales almost exactly as sqrt(score) ~ steps
  (median exponent 0.499), and the 608 BB(6) list is similar (0.478).
  That is descriptive, not a proof of counter/bouncer behaviour.
- The extra 23 formal BB(2,5) machines are mostly shallow; they lower the
  60-list median but do not change the hard core.
- Raw per-run outputs are in deep_test/ (608 at 10^8),
  deep_bb6_specs_1000000000/, deep_37_1e8/, deep_37_1e10/,
  deep_60_1e9/, and deep_60_1e10/.  results.csv is generated and ignored
  by git; summary.txt is the compact evidence.
